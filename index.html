Musically Trompette

Practice your scales, strengthen your musical knowledge, and build confidence on trumpet.

Scale Builder

Build scales one note at a time. Choose a key, scale, and range, then test your knowledge of the notes and fingerings.

Scale Identification

Look at a sequence of notes and identify the scale. Practice recognizing scale patterns and key signatures.

High Note Identification

Identify notes well above the staff. Practice reading ledger lines and recognizing higher pitches.

Low Note Identification

Identify notes well below the staff. Practice reading ledger lines and recognizing lower pitches.
